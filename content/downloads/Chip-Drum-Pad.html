Hardware

Each console has only a few sound channels. With real chip channels on, a Game Boy hi-hat stops the snare that's still ringing on the noise channel, and the speech chip can only say one thing at a time. Turn it off to let every hit ring out.

Buttons

Make a layout to give any pad any console's drum, or an action like Play, Save 60s, next pattern or tap tempo. Normal keeps the pads as they are.

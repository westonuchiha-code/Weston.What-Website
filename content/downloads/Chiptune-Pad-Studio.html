Pattern

Tap a square to add or remove a hit. Each tap on a bass step moves it up one note; tap past the top note to clear it. With chords picked, the bass moves to each bar's chord and the pads ring the notes of the chord that's playing, so you know what fits. Tempo, key and scale come from the key and tempo menu.

      Draw your wave

      Drag your finger to draw. When you pick My Wave, the Game Boy, Game Boy Color and WonderSwan play this shape on their wave channels, which hold 32 steps at 16 levels on the real hardware too. Smooth shapes sound soft; jagged ones sound buzzy. Each song keeps its own wave.

      The MP3 (192 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo) is saved inside a .zip file, because this viewer can't save .mp3 files directly. Tap the .zip in your Files app to unzip it.

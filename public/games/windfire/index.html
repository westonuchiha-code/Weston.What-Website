STICK: fly the wisp (push gently to drift, hard to speed up). Fly through flames to carry embers to new buildings. Burning buildings fills your heat bar; at each level pick an upgrade (stick up/down, A). The run ends when the fire dies out. Ash earned buys permanent upgrades between runs. B: dash (once you pick the Dash upgrade). START: begin / pause. SELECT: new city. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Z or Space = A, Enter = START, Shift = SELECT.
